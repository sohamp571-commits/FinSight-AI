# FinSight AI — User Manual

_This manual covers the features available today (Authentication & Account Management). It will grow as later phases add the dashboard, portfolio, and analytics screens._

## Creating an Account

1. Open the app and click **Create an account**.
2. Fill in your full name, email, username, and a password.
   - Passwords must be at least 8 characters and include at least one letter and one digit.
3. Accept the Terms of Service and click **Create Account**.
4. You'll be returned to the Login page — sign in with your new username or email.

## Logging In

1. Enter your **username or email** and **password**.
2. Optionally check **Remember me for 30 days** to stay logged in longer.
3. Click **Log In**.

If you enter the wrong password five times in a row, your account will be temporarily locked for security — wait a while and try again, or reset your password.

## Forgot Your Password?

1. On the Login page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address on your account and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. You'll see a confirmation message either way (this is intentional, for your security).
4. Follow the link/token to the Reset Password page, choose a new password, and confirm it.

Reset links expire after 30 minutes and can only be used once.

## Changing Your Password

While logged in, open **Change Password** from the sidebar, enter your current password, then your new password twice, and click **Update Password**.

## Managing Your Profile

Open **My Profile** from the sidebar to:
- View your account status, role, member-since date, and last login
- Update your full name and email (your username cannot be changed)
- Upload a profile picture (PNG/JPG/JPEG/WEBP, up to 2MB)
- Review your recent login activity (success/failure and IP address)

## Logging Out

Click **Log Out** in the sidebar at any time. This ends your session immediately.

## Account Status

- **Active** accounts can log in normally.
- **Inactive** accounts have been deactivated (by you or an admin) and cannot log in until reactivated.
- If your account is deleted, it is soft-deleted — your historical data is preserved for records/compliance, but you can no longer log in.

## Getting Help

If something isn't working as expected, check the **Troubleshooting** section of the project `README.md`, or `docs/deployment.md` for common startup issues.
